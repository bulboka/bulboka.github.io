In the first part of the game, the player could choose whose ear to crawl into — the man's or the woman's. The Mysterious Beast accordingly crawled into the ear of the other person. For simplicity, let's assume Martin crawled into the man, and the Mysterious Beast into the woman.

So, on the island they both gradually grow old. When they've both grown very old, the Woman trips once again and calls for help. The player walks up to her and presses the action button, which usually resulted in the Man helping the Woman get up. But this time a cutscene starts. In the foreground there's still the brown frame and the small sleeping Martin floating around, but in front of the man's eyes is a close-up of the woman, now completely aged, lying on the ground. The Man is holding her hand. A dialogue begins between them; the player only presses to advance to the next line, with no choices. During the dialogue, the small sleeping Martin slowly sinks to the very bottom of the screen and stops floating around the screen.

W: Didn't work out, huh?
M: What didn't work out?
W: (smiling) I don't know. It just feels like something didn't work out.
M: I haven't got a fucking clue what you're talking about.
W: Me neither. (pause) Now... (winces, as if in pain, pause) hold on... (winces harder, pause... a loud, sharp fart sound is heard)

At the moment of the sound, the small sleeping Martin abruptly opens his eyes. He keeps lying there, but stares straight ahead with a bewildered look.

The Woman starts cackling; a moment later the Man joins in (the camera shakes up and down), the creaky sound of old people's laughter. They laugh for a long time.

M: Now that's interesting!

They laugh a bit more. During this, Martin has sat up, but is still staring ahead of him like someone who hasn't fully woken up.

M: No, seriously though. Is this what you'd want to do in what might be your final minutes?
W: Well, no. It just happened.
M: It just happened... (pause) Yeah... (pause) Sorry.
W: Oh, come on, cut it out.

The Man and the Woman continue the dialogue in the same vein — I haven't finished writing this part yet. Meanwhile, Martin livens up a little, starts moving his pupils slightly. Then he slaps one thigh, then the other, as if he can't find something in his pockets. Eventually he finds it, pulls out a cigarette and lights up; smoke rises.

Meanwhile, the Woman, in the middle of yet another line, winces again just like the first time, then her whole body relaxes, she closes her eyes and no longer speaks or moves.

M: Hey. Hey, what's wrong? Come on, what is it? Hey! Hey! Hey! What's wrong with you?

He just keeps repeating the same thing. At this point the smoke from Martin's cigarette completely fills the screen. Martin coughs. End of game.
